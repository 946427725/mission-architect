# Demo video shot list (30 seconds or less, a hard rule)

Space Apps rule: the demo must be **30 seconds or less** (or at most 7 slides), or the project is not eligible for global judging. Aim for 28 s. English captions burned in.

**Record with a repeatable link** so the same events happen every take:
`http://localhost:5173/?mission=mars&preset=nasa&seed=1&speed=4`
(Seed 1: a relay orbiter fails and the high-gain dish saves the mission, and a global dust storm is survived thanks to the nuclear battery. Result: Mission success, APPROVED stamp.)

Record at 1920×1080, Chrome fullscreen (F11), 60 fps. Use real gameplay; motion graphics go on top. Use a human voice-over and no AI-generated images (safest under the AI-disclosure rules). No NASA logos.

| Time | Shot | How to capture | Caption / voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Title screen, solar system turning | open the plain URL, wait | "Mission Architect: design a space mission. Real physics decides." |
| 3-8 s | Route step → **Check the physics**: the real Perseverance launch pinned on the chart | Improve the design → Route → Check the physics | "Every pixel is a trajectory from NASA/JPL planet positions. Mars 2020: 14.4 vs NASA's 14.5." |
| 8-13 s | Craft/Science step: switch power, add an instrument; gauges flash "+15 kg", the 3D rover changes | any design | "Every choice costs mass, power, money and data." |
| 13-16 s | Launch step: GO poll, press and hold → 3-2-1 → LIFTOFF | demo link | "Pass the GO/NO-GO poll. Hold to launch." |
| 16-22 s | Cruise at 4×, landing sequence ENTRY → PARACHUTE → SKY CRANE → TOUCHDOWN, dust-storm card "Saved by: Nuclear power" | demo link | "Events from real missions test your margins." |
| 22-27 s | Report: APPROVED stamp, "One change that would have helped most", You vs NASA | demo link | "Learn why, find the change that helps most, compare with NASA." |
| 27-28 s | End card: name, URL, team | motion graphic | NASA Space Apps 2026 · [team name] |

Also record a longer 2-minute walkthrough for the project page (not as the demo link).
